Vocabulary namespace v0.1

Draft. Expect breaking changes until v1.0.

https://spec.bidangelai.com/v0.1/ is the namespace IRI of the A2A Procurement Profile v0.1 vocabulary. The JSON-LD contexts bind it to the a2a: prefix and use it as @vocab.

Only terms with no counterpart in UBL 2.3, Peppol BIS Pre-Award, OCDS 1.1.5 or EU eForms are defined here. Terms that map to an existing vocabulary keep that vocabulary's IRI.
